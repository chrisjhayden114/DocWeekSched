Note on AERA SIGs: no standalone SIG-level conferences in this window were found with verifiable pages — but most rows below (NRMERA, MWERA, NERA, MSERA, SERA, EERA, FERA, CERA) are AERA state/regional affiliates, which was the intent of that ask.
